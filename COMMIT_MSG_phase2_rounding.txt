Phase 2 task 1: scenarios round — multiplier moves to per-cycle amounts

Implements demand_expansion_scope_and_site_capacity.md 3.2.5, locked 2026-09-19
and not built until now, plus handoff open item #2. Closes handoff items #1 and
#2.

THE DEFECT

scenario.py applied recipe_input_multiplier to rate_per_min. The game applies it
to a recipe's per-cycle integer part counts and rounds to the nearest whole
number, per input — confirmed in game at 1.25x in both directions on 1.2.4.0
CL#502094 (record 3.2.4). Rounding is not expressible on a rate, so this was not
a one-line fix: the transform point had to move.

THE CONTRACT CHANGE

  Recipe            gains input_amounts: (item, amount_per_cycle, unit)
  Recipe.scaled()   scales and rounds the per-cycle amounts, then re-derives
                    the per-minute rates
  Scenario          apply_input_rate REMOVED, not deprecated. Leaving a
                    rate-shaped input transform in place is the defect; its
                    absence is the tripwire, so a missed call site raises
                    AttributeError rather than returning an unrounded answer
                    apply_input_amount(amount_per_cycle, unit) replaces it
                    apply_output_rate unchanged — outputs neither scale nor round

lp_backend, analysis and contracts still read Recipe.inputs as rates and are not
touched.

At recipe_input_multiplier == 1.0 the canonical rates are carried through rather
than re-derived. A power-only scenario must not perturb inputs, and
Recipe_Battery_C draws 2.5 m3 of Sulfuric Acid — a 1x round trip would round it
to 3 and invent a cost the game does not charge.

Checked while wiring it: amount_per_cycle * 60 / manufacturing_duration_sec
equals rate_per_min on all 919 rows of recipe_io.csv, to 1e-6.

BEHAVIOUR CHANGE — declared, with numbers

  Iron Plate input at 1.25x           37.5/min  ->  40.0/min
  Iron Plate 20/min -> iron ore         46.875  ->  40.00
  Smart Plating 1/min -> iron ore       62.408  ->  33.50
  Iron Plate 20/min, power at 2x       18.0 MW  ->  18.667 MW

Smart Plating falls 46%. Of the six recipes on that chain, three round away
entirely: Smart Plating's own 1 RIP and 1 Rotor stay at 1 (the in-game read),
and Iron Rod's and Iron Ingot's single input rounds 1.25 back to 1, so the chain
stops compounding at two stages instead of five. Only Reinforced Iron Plate
(6 -> 8 plate, 12 -> 15 screw), Rotor (5 -> 6 rod, 25 -> 31 screw) and Iron
Plate (3 -> 4 ingot) move at all.

Direction and rough magnitude agree with the -28% the record measured on
lifetime Modular Frame demand — corroboration from an independent chain.

production_solver_selection.md 3.3 is NOT edited. Its 46.88 and 62.41 described
the adapter accurately when written. Supersession is recorded forward, in the
test docstrings that carried those constants and in the implementation note.

WIDER THAN 3.2.5 ANTICIPATED

3.2.5 expected a contract change at one method. The scalar input_multiplier
parameter is also carried by both independent checkers under tests/ —
_demand_oracle.demand() and _balance_check.violations() — each multiplying it
into a rate, so both were wrong in the same way as the adapter.

Both keep the parameter name and type; only its application changed. Each
reimplements the rule from recipe_io.csv rather than importing
production_adapter.scenario, preserving the independence their docstrings
require. Three copies of the rule now exist, deliberately.

They agree. The oracle and the LP backend land on 33.50 and 40.00 from opposite
directions, to the digit, and a test now pins that so a future divergence fails
rather than passing quietly.

DECLARED RATHER THAN ASSUMED

  FLUID_ROUNDING_UNIT   NEW OPEN ITEM. 3.2.5 adopts the same rule for fluids but
                        does not say in which unit, and m3 and mL disagree on
                        every fluid input: 3 m3 x 1.25 is 4 m3 rounding in m3
                        and 3.75 m3 rounding in mL. 3.2.3's A-D agreement was
                        Modular Frame, which has no fluid in the chain, so it
                        does not transfer. Named at one module constant,
                        defaulting to m3. Handoff item #3 now settles the unit
                        as well as the rule.

  input_amount_floor    Scenario refuses to construct below 1x without it. The
                        record leaves the floor unresolved and says sub-1x
                        figures stay open; refusing to compute them is the
                        faithful encoding, and a silent zero input is the
                        failure mode 1.2.3.0 actually shipped. Cannot bind at
                        1x or above.

Also recorded: 3.2.2's computation rounded items only, while 3.2.5 as adopted
rounds fluids too. Both give 66,423 for the scenario of record because those
paths carry no fluid, so the divergence has been invisible. The code follows
3.2.5, which is the decision.

MARGINAL_PEAK_DEBOTTLENECK

Added, 1.25 / 2.0 / 2.0, distinct from CHALLENGE_1_25X_2X which carries a 1.0
Project Assembly multiplier and is a different save.

No CLI preset for it, deliberately. The production solver does not consume the
Project Assembly multiplier, so --scenario record would behave identically to
--scenario challenge while appearing to apply the setting it is named for. Worth
adding when the Phase 2 scheduler consumes that multiplier, not before.
